Lyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Lyra Japan, operated by Edge of Vision ("we"), handles information used by Lyra and Lyra Mobile (the "Service") as described here.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address, Cognito user ID, display name, and workspace membership.
  • User content such as stories, characters, pages, dialogue, instructions, and uploaded images.
  • Purchased products, transaction status, and credit history. We do not hold complete payment-card numbers.
  • Device, OS, app version, crash, diagnostics, and security records. We do not track users for advertising.

How we use information

We use information for authentication, editing, AI generation, storage and export, purchase verification, support, abuse prevention, service safety and quality, and legal compliance.

AI and service providers

When a user explicitly starts an AI operation, the required story text, settings, or image is sent to OpenAI. The app identifies the recipient and data before transmission and asks for consent. We use Amazon Web Services for hosting, storage, delivery, and authentication; Apple, Google, or Stripe for purchase processing and verification; and Sentry for privacy-limited crash diagnostics and in-app reports about AI-generated content (a fixed category, reason, and, when available, an opaque content record ID). These providers process data under their terms and safeguards. Service delivery may involve international processing or transfers.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We disclose it only to the service providers described above, with consent, for a business transfer, or where required by law.

Retention and security

We retain information only as needed to provide the Service, administer contracts, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with law, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards including encryption in transit, access controls, and separation of secrets.

Your choices and rights

You can edit information, decline an AI transfer, and delete your account in the app. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. See Account deletion for steps and retention exceptions.

Age

The Service is intended for users aged 17 or older.
