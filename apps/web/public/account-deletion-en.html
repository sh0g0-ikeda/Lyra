Delete your Lyra account

In the app: Settings (Account) → Delete account → review the impact → complete required acknowledgements and enter "DELETE" → start deletion.

If you cannot use the app, send a request from your registered email address with subject "Lyra account deletion request" to lyra.japan.official@gmail.com. We will verify ownership before processing.

Data deleted

We delete or anonymize personal works, personal images, personal workspace settings, device tokens, and direct identifiers. Completed deletion cannot be reversed.

Before deletion

  • Finish or stop active generation and export jobs.
  • A sole organization owner must transfer ownership first.
  • Cancel Apple or Google subscriptions in the relevant store. Deleting the app or Lyra account may not cancel a store subscription automatically.

Limited retention

Transaction and audit records required for law, accounting, refunds, fraud prevention, security, or disputes may be retained with reduced identifiability for the necessary period. Organization works, billing, and audit records remain with that organization while the user identity is anonymized.
